Clepeus Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Clepeus is a browser extension created by Belmont Labs Inc. It warns you about scam messages and about hidden instructions aimed at AI assistants. This policy explains what the extension does with your information.

The short version

Clepeus checks pages on your own computer. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your browsing activity or the content of the pages you visit. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the extension reads

To do its job, the extension reads the text, links, and page code of the web pages you open, including messages you open in webmail. It compares them against a fixed list of warning signs that is built into the extension. This check happens entirely inside your browser. The page content is not saved and never leaves your computer.

What the extension stores

The extension stores two settings in your browser's local extension storage:

  • whether checking is turned on or off, and
  • the list of sites you have chosen to trust.

These settings stay on your computer. You can change them in the extension's popup, and they are deleted when you remove the extension.

What the extension sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests and loads no remote code.

Sharing with third parties

No information is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
